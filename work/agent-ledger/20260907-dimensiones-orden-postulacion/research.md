## Synthesis

The strongest defensible thesis is not that the project discovers a new law of perception. It is that the work exposes and tests a translation problem: a deterministic, continuous phase rule produces a rich simulated field, while physical devices receive discrete, delayed samples. The artistic result is the comparison itself; the scientific discipline comes from recording the rule, perturbations, timing, and deviations.

The precedent review strengthens the software framing. The line has already selected works whose audiovisual identity involves immersive installation, interaction, mixed reality, performance, animation and alternative formats. Therefore Blender, TouchDesigner and Avolites can be presented as a production chain only if they serve one identifiable audiovisual work. Blender generates the procedural visual field; TouchDesigner translates the state in real time and can carry audio/OSC/DMX; Avolites materializes a reduced state as physical light. The application should not present three software demonstrations or a lighting-control product. It should present one audiovisual work with two manifestations: a fixed audiovisual composition and a live/physical translation whose deviations are part of the experimentation.

## Stopping rationale

The remaining research would not close the main risks. The decision now depends more on correcting local evidence, replacing stale claims, classifying labor lines correctly, obtaining quotations, and completing the official platform fields than on collecting more theory. The official bases make coherence, feasibility, creative proposal and curriculum the material evaluation levers, so the next effort should be evidence and submission hygiene rather than additional conceptual expansion.

### Decision update

The project is now framed as a live audiovisual work with a shared semantic light state, not as a Blender-to-BLE comparison alone. Layout discovery, patch verification and protocol translation are part of the composition because a sequence over physical neighbors is not equivalent to a sequence over channel order. XIO is an experimental mapping/translation instrument inside the work; a future open-source or commercial line may generalize its intermediate representation, fixture profiles and adapters, but those future capabilities are not claimed as complete deliverables here.

### Synthesis update

The same semantic light system can support a portfolio of applications, but the result cannot be copied unchanged. The audiovisual route asks for a work and process document; the creation route asks for a situated work and exhibition; the diffusion route asks for circulation of an existing work; the formative route asks for transfer to participants; the research route asks for a field study and knowledge transfer. The new matrix records these boundaries and prevents the future product layers (XIO, LUCIDA, VIZZ, PUPILA and IRIS) from being claimed as current funded deliverables.
