## Strongest failure mode

The proposal could present a partial render range as if it were the whole work, or present a single tested BLE device as proof of a multi-device installation. Either error would weaken admissibility and credibility more than a missing stylistic paragraph.

## Alternatives considered

- Continue polishing the existing treatment: low effort, but preserves stale counts and confuses implemented evidence with future work.
- Rebuild from the verified camera and Geometry Nodes evidence: moderate effort, directly reduces the largest credibility risk.
- Add more external research before writing: useful only for the scientific rationale; not a substitute for correcting local evidence.

## Selection

Rebuild the dossier with a paired simulation/physical experiment, explicit measurements, and a conservative evidence register. External research is limited to sources that affect synchrony, procedural reproducibility, or the call's eligibility.

verification_signal: MP4 frame count equals the full camera keyframe interval 300-790 at 30 FPS; stale line counts are corrected in the dossier.

## Checkpoint 15 critique

Objective: make the application represent the user's actual center of gravity: a common mathematical audiovisual layer that lets spatial and temporal intentions travel across heterogeneous lighting/video systems, with XIO mapping and verifying the venue rather than acting as an inflated universal product claim.

Strongest failure mode: preserve the old narrow framing and describe the work as a BLE synchronization test. That would make the camera mapping, autopatching, console-translation and LED-surface ideas look like unrelated additions and would understate the artistic problem.

Alternative considered: rewrite the application as a commercial lighting controller. Rejected because it weakens fit with an experimental audiovisual call, overclaims the current evidence and creates a much larger product-validation burden.

Selected action: change_method. The dossier now uses a semantic intermediate state, explicit mapping/patch separation, preshow/soundcheck/liveshow phases, adapter capability declarations, and new measurements for mapping accuracy, patch recovery, translation loss and safe state.

Evidence for the change: official RDM, Open Lighting Architecture, Open Fixture Library and TouchDesigner documentation support the feasibility of the component boundaries; they do not prove the final system. The official OUTWORLD material supports the artistic precedent of light, sound and architecture, not the proposed technical method.

Verification signal: revised proposal, treatment, method, FUP blocks, jury brief and annex must remain coherent; the local package verifier and PDF render checks must pass. Reverse the decision only if the official FUP cannot accommodate the work as an audiovisual result or if the final budget/venue cannot support the controlled prototype.
