# FARMAKSIA public scope and licensing policy

This repository is the public research archive and compatibility surface of
FARMAKSIA. The existing project history and the original FARMAKSIA material
remain distributed under the MIT License in `LICENSE` unless a file or
subdirectory states a different license.

This public archive does not contain the private product cores of:

- IRIS — interactive portfolio application;
- PUPILA — interface association and analogical transfer application;
- VIZZ — eye-tracking and dual-camera geometry engine.

Those products live in their own private repositories. A future public module
will declare its license in its own directory and source headers; no future
license change should be inferred from this archive automatically.

## Third-party material

Third-party code and assets retain their original licenses. The historical
WebGazer dependency was removed from the active tree after its experiment was
retired; its license and technical assessment remain recorded in the research
and decision documents. No WebGazer bundle is shipped by the current tree.

## Names and product rights

The names FARMAKSIA, IRIS, PUPILA, and VIZZ are project/product identifiers;
this software license does not grant trademark rights. Publishing research
does not grant permission to use the private product implementations,
private archives, user material, credentials, or MAK internal files.
