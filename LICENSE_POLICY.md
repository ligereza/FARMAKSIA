# FARMAKSIA research and licensing boundary

FARMAKSIA is the research archive: documents, datasets with documented
rights, hypotheses, reproducible experiments and evidence. It is not the
authoritative runtime for the shared engine or host integrations.

The active project boundaries are:

- `X-ANA-X`: shared engine and canonical contracts.
- `PUPILA`: assistance and perceptual/visual reasoning.
- `LUCIDA`: application-specific projections and host integrations.
- `FARMAKSIA`: research records and experiments; it contributes evidence,
  not an unreviewed product implementation.

The root `LICENSE` applies unless a file or subdirectory declares otherwise.
Third-party code and assets retain their original licenses. Do not add a
vendored dependency, private product implementation, personal data, credentials
or MAK-only material without checking its provenance and rights first. A
research adapter or synthetic fixture does not transfer ownership or licensing
of the source application.
