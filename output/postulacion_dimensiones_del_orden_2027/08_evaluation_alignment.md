# Evaluation alignment

The official bases evaluate three criteria: technical-financial proposal 40%, curriculum 20%, and creative proposal 40%. The project should be written so that each criterion can be scored from the same evidence instead of repeating a generic description.

## Technical-financial proposal - 40%

The strongest argument is the dependency between activity, risk and measurement. The budget buys the physical test and the translation layer, not generic equipment: each device, hour and operation line is connected to layout reconstruction, patch verification, semantic translation and the simulation-to-hardware comparison. The schedule proceeds from reference scene, to one-device isolation, to geometric mapping, to multi-device test, to recording and process document. The treatment identifies a real venue requirement and a circulation plan, but a concrete venue or documented contact would strengthen viability.

Weakness to close: the original personal subtotal was too close to the 20%
limit, and the BLE unit price is an estimate. A reconciled local variant now
sets Personal at 18.78%; it still needs final labor classification and at least
one quotation. Those are higher-value improvements than adding more conceptual
language.

## Curriculum - 20%

The application must show that the responsible person can execute both sides of the project: audiovisual composition and technical implementation. The relevant evidence is not a list of every project. It is a small set of dated works that demonstrates procedural 3D, audiovisual production, live visual systems, code-driven media, and documentation. Profile Cultura is the authoritative submission surface; this dossier cannot substitute for it.

## Creative proposal - 40%

The project is strongest when the experiment is also the artwork. The visual language comes from the phase field and spatial graph; the physical deviation, patch discrepancy and translation loss are not hidden as defects. LED surfaces are treated as luminous architecture rather than merely as image playback. The complementary MP4 is a prototype of the procedural layer, while the funded project expands the work through mapping, semantic translation, controlled physical execution and a process document. This makes the audiovisual material evidence of the proposed method rather than an unrelated reel.

## Score strategy

Do not add VIZZ, IRIS, LUCIDA or other ecosystem projects to this application. They may appear as background to the author's practice only if the official form permits it, but they are not part of the committed work. Mixing them would reduce coherence and make the project look like software development instead of a bounded audiovisual experiment.
