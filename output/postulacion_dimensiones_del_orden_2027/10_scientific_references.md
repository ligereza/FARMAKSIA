## How they are used

The references are used as methodological support, not as evidence that this
project has already produced a general perceptual result.

| Source | What it supports here | What it does not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Van der Burg et al. (2014) | Measuring transient audiovisual events and separating temporal correspondence from spatial clutter; the study found a finite synchrony window and strong effects of transient waveform design | A universal tolerance for this installation, or a claim about every viewer |
| Parise et al. (2026) | Pre-registering temporal sampling, distinguishing simultaneity from temporal-order tasks, and reporting method dependence rather than importing one canonical threshold | A ready-made pass/fail threshold for BLE or for this artwork |
| Park (2011) | Treating a procedural rule as a reproducible artistic material and documenting its generative structure | Proof that this project's specific Geometry Nodes graph is historically or scientifically equivalent |
| Official 2027 bases | Defining the eligible experimental audiovisual result, process document and submission constraints | Scientific validation of the artistic method |
| ESTA E1.20 RDM | Supporting the distinction between bidirectional device discovery/configuration and ordinary DMX output | Proof that every fixture in the proposed venue supports RDM or that RDM alone can identify physical position |
| Open Lighting Architecture | Supporting the use of an interoperability layer over DMX/RDM and related protocols | Proof that the project's semantic state or camera mapping already works |
| Open Fixture Library | Supporting the reuse of structured fixture profiles and conversion instead of building every fixture definition from zero | Complete coverage of Chinese or proprietary fixtures, or automatic correctness of a profile |
| TouchDesigner documentation | Supporting the practical routing of channels, audio and protocol data through a live visual runtime | Proof of the final TouchDesigner patch or of artistic success |
| OUTWORLD official presentation | Supporting an artistic precedent where architecture, light and sound form one live experience | Evidence of OUTWORLD's internal software, autopatching or technical architecture |

The audiovisual synchrony sources therefore justify explicit timing
measurements and warn against treating a command timestamp as a perceptual
result. The procedural-art source supports treating the rule as part of the
work's material, not only as an implementation detail. The official bases
define the admissibility and evaluation constraints.

The project uses those sources conservatively: it reports the behaviour of a
specified rule under a specified material translation, while leaving any
broader perceptual interpretation as a question for the resulting work and
its documented observation. The interoperability sources also justify a
practical division: RDM and fixture profiles can assist discovery, while
camera geometry and active probing address the remaining physical mapping
problem.
