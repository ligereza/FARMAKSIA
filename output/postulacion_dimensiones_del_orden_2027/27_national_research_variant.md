# National research variant

## Working title

Cartografia de la luz entre sistemas: interfaces, protocolos y espacios de trabajo audiovisual en Chile

## Status

This is a separate research proposal draft for Fondart Nacional 2027, Group A: visual arts, photography, new media or interdiscipline. It is not the production proposal. Its main result must be field knowledge and transfer, not a finished installation.

## Research problem

When a live audiovisual work moves between LED surfaces, physical luminaires, software and consoles, operators often rebuild the same spatial and temporal intention through incompatible vocabularies. The technical problem is also a cultural and design problem: which parts of the work are encoded in the interface, which remain in the operator's body and memory, and which are lost when a venue or device changes?

## Research question

How do artists, lighting operators, VJs, designers and venues in Chile describe, transfer and verify spatial light intentions when working across screens, luminaires, consoles, protocols and different physical layouts?

## Field design

The study will map a bounded sample of artists, operators, venues and technical configurations. It will combine semi-structured interviews, observation of setup and soundcheck processes, document analysis of showfiles or patchfiles when participants authorize it, and comparative reconstruction of a small set of light intentions. The study will distinguish self-reported practice, observed workflow, device capability and the researcher's interpretation.

The analysis will use a common vocabulary of phase, tempo, energy, density, direction, topology, palette, latency, mapping, patch and translation loss. This vocabulary is an analytic instrument, not a claim that all practices are equivalent.

## Outputs

- A public research report with methodology, anonymized findings and limitations.
- A map of recurring interface, protocol and spatial problems.
- A comparative taxonomy of how operators recover from layout, patch and timing uncertainty.
- A public presentation or workshop transferring the findings.
- A small open methodological annex that does not expose private showfiles or personal data.

## Separation from the production work

The live work can serve as a case study or pilot instrument, but it cannot replace the field sample. The research will not claim that the prototype represents the Chilean sector. Conversely, the production application will not promise to complete this national study.

## Bases alignment

The call requires field studies that contribute to knowledge in the eligible disciplines. For Group A, new media and interdisciplinary research are eligible. It requires authorization to publish at least a summary and a two-page research text for knowledge transfer. The maximum amount is CLP 15,000,000, the project must begin between 1 March and 30 April 2027, and 5% to 10% of the requested funds may be allocated to the knowledge-transfer plan.
