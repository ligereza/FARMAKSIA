# Formative activity variant

## Working title

Luz entre sistemas: laboratory of spatial light and interface translation

## Status

This is a separate draft for Fondart Regional 2027, Formative Activities. It is not a production application. It requires a collective project structure, a participant profile, a free activity and a concrete transfer plan.

## Need

Artists and technical operators often know one application or console but face difficulty when a work moves to another interface, protocol or physical layout. The difficulty is not only vocabulary. It is the loss of a shared model of phase, rhythm, energy, density, distance and spatial relation.

## Formative proposal

The project is a free laboratory in which participants build a small spatial graph, generate a procedural light state, map a controlled set of sources or LED surfaces and compare native control with a semantic relation layer. Participants learn to express a light intention before choosing a software or console, then observe what survives, degrades or diverges when the intention reaches a concrete device.

## Learning outcomes

Participants will be able to:

- describe a light action as spatial and temporal relations;
- distinguish layout mapping from DMX or protocol patching;
- create a bounded procedural reference in Blender or TouchDesigner;
- test a small physical or simulated configuration;
- document latency, missing capabilities, translation loss and recovery from error;
- transfer the same intention to a different interface without treating the interface as the concept itself.

## Activity structure

The laboratory includes an introduction to the common semantic state, a procedural exercise, a layout exercise, a controlled mapping and patch test, a group comparison and a final public demonstration. The activity is free and does not lead to an academic degree.

## Evidence of transfer

Each group produces a small state file, a spatial diagram, a record of the native and translated versions, and a short reflection describing what was preserved and what changed. The final public demonstration makes the transfer visible instead of evaluating participants only through a questionnaire.

## Bases alignment

The call funds collective projects that transfer knowledge, techniques and practices through workshops, laboratories, residencies, workshops, tutorials or mentoring. The activity must be free, must not lead to an academic degree, must begin between 1 April and 31 May 2027, and may last up to 12 months. The maximum project amount is CLP 15,000,000; the responsible person's assignment may reach 40% and unforeseen expenses may not exceed 2%.
