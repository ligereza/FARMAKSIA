# Diffusion variant

## Working title

Dimensiones del Orden: circulacion de una obra luminosa entre pantalla y espacio

## Status

This is a downstream draft for Fondart Regional 2027, Diffusion. It becomes eligible as a coherent proposal only after the work exists as a created piece and there is a credible public circulation route.

## Project statement

The project circulates an already-created live audiovisual work in which LED surfaces, sound and physical luminaires form a luminous architecture. The diffusion strategy presents the work through a public installation or live performance, an accessible audiovisual record, a short explanation of the translation process and a documented public encounter.

The central public question is not how to operate a console. It is how one mathematical light relation changes when it moves between a simulated field, a screen surface and a physical space. The diffusion package makes the work accessible without reducing it to a technical demonstration.

## Public strategy

- one confirmed presentation or exhibition route;
- one online or local audiovisual record with accessible description;
- a mediation text explaining the relation between sound, surface and spatial light;
- documentation of audience access and encounter;
- a plan for communication, circulation and public development.

## What it cannot finance in this form

This variant should not ask the diffusion line to invent the work, buy the first experimental hardware configuration or develop the mapping layer from zero. Those belong to creation or production. The technical archive is supporting evidence of portability and authorship, not the main project result.

## Bases alignment

The call is for works already created and asks for a detailed diffusion plan, public circulation and audience development. It allows in-person or mixed formats and recognizes immersive experiences, online exhibition, mobile applications and other technology-enabled formats. The maximum amount is CLP 18,000,000; the project must begin between 1 April and 31 May 2027, last up to 12 months, and unforeseen expenses may not exceed 2%.
