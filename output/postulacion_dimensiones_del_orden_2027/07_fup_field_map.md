# FUP field map

This map separates copy-ready project content from applicant-only fields. It is a preparation aid, not a substitute for the official form.

| Official field family | Use from this dossier | Applicant action |
|---|---|---|
| Project title | `Dimensiones del Orden` | Confirm exact spelling. |
| Artistic proposal | `01_project_proposal.md` and section 1-2 of `06_treatment_rewrite.md` | Paste and adapt to character limit. |
| Experimental method | `02_experimental_method.md` and section 4 of the treatment | Paste the shortened version required by the platform. |
| Work and outputs | Proposal outputs and treatment section 8 | Confirm final delivery format. |
| Technical feasibility | Evidence register plus risk section and semantic interoperability annex | Attach quotations, fixture/protocol specification and venue feasibility evidence. |
| Schedule | Treatment section 6 | Convert to the platform's month/date fields. |
| Budget | `04_budget_audit.md` plus final official table | Recalculate after quotations and caps. |
| Curriculum | External CV and Profile Cultura | Applicant must verify and submit. |
| Rights and permissions | Evidence register and gate | Confirm assets, audio, textures, fonts, venue and any protocol/fixture resources. |
| Region, address and eligibility | Not included here | Applicant-only. |

## Copy rule

Do not copy the evidence register verbatim into the artistic proposal. Use it to prevent overclaiming. The proposal should read as an artwork and experiment; the register remains the audit layer.
