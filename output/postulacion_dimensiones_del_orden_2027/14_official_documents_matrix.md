# Official documents matrix

This matrix follows Annex 2 of the official 2027 bases. It distinguishes
documents that can make the application inadmissible from documents that
affect evaluation. It is a preparation aid, not a substitute for the
platform. Internal audit files, research notes and jury briefs are not
additional submission attachments unless the FUP explicitly requests them.

## Documents required for evaluation

| Document | Current status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Budget form | Working arithmetic audited; official form not filled | Transfer the final values to the official 2027 form and include all project stages, contributions and requested amounts. This document is required for the line. |
| Audiovisual treatment folder | Drafted and rendered in `submission_treatment.pdf` | Adapt to the platform or official attachment convention; keep within 20 pages. This document is required for evaluation. |
| Complementary audiovisual material | HD derived MP4 available | Upload the platform-safe copy only after confirming the platform accepts the chosen codec and dimensions; keep the source hash in the archive. Maximum duration is 5 minutes. |

## Minimum documents when applicable

| Condition | Document | Current status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protected third-party works or related rights are used | Copyright authorization | Not determined | Confirm audio, textures, fonts, footage, logos and other protected material; attach authorization when required. |
| Responsible party is a private for-profit legal entity | Ownership and administration identification | Not applicable unless legal entity is selected | Attach the required names and RUT information. |
| The project has essential team members | Team commitment letters | Not determined | Identify only essential roles and obtain signed letters for each. |
| Activities occur in territory inhabited by an Indigenous community | Community consent letter | Not determined | Attach only if the project actually uses such a territory. |
| Responsible party is a private legal entity | Statutes or incorporation record | Not applicable unless legal entity is selected | Confirm that the corporate purpose covers the project. |
| Direct work with minors is planned | Certificate of ineligibility to work with minors | Not applicable in the current treatment | Add only if the project is changed to include direct work with minors. |

## Admissibility rule

The FUP must be complete, the selected fund must be pertinent, the project dates and applicant eligibility must comply, all applicable minimum documents must be attached, and the submission must be sent before the applicable deadline. Missing an applicable taxative document can place the application outside the call.

Do not attach the full internal dossier as a substitute for the official
documents. The platform and the FAQ distinguish taxative minimum documents
from internal preparation material; upload only what the FUP and Annex 2
request.
