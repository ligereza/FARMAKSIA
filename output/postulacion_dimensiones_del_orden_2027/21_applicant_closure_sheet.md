# Applicant closure sheet

> **Current successor of:** [`17_source_replacement_plan.md`](17_source_replacement_plan.md). See [`README.md`](README.md).

This sheet is the final handoff between the prepared dossier and the official
application platform. It contains no applicant-only personal data and should be
completed by the applicant immediately before submission. Budget, venue, rights,
quotations and platform state also require evidence; they are not merely personal-data fields.

## Transfer order

1. Confirm call, region and deadline on the platform.
2. Complete applicant and project identity fields.
3. Paste the proposal and method from the local dossier.
4. Enter the reconciled budget in the official form and attach quotations.
5. Attach the treatment and complementary video individually.
6. Add only the conditional documents that actually apply.
7. Compare title, amount, dates, duration, device count, rights and outputs
   across every field and attachment.
8. Submit only when the platform reports no mandatory-field error.
9. Save the receipt, folio, date and time in the local dossier.

Official deadline source checked on 2026-09-07:
https://www.fondosdecultura.cl/area/audiovisual/produccion-de-obras-experimentales-fondo-audiovisual-2027/

- Do not describe four or six synchronized BLE devices as an existing result.
- Do not turn the scientific references into a claim of medical or universal
  perceptual effect.

  the official project scope is deliberately rewritten and re-evaluated.
