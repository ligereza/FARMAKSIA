## Admissibility risks

The original Personal allocation is too close to the 20% ceiling. The local
reconciled variant lowers it to 18.78%; recalculate it after quotations and
labor classification before upload.

The five BLE luminaires are still estimates. Distinguish the already owned device from the units to be purchased, and replace the unit estimate with at least one quotation or a defensible calculation and compatible specification.

The audiovisual registration line must state whether equipment is owned, rented or subcontracted. Do not charge the same cost as both personal labor and operation. The track, venue, rights, travel and support materials must appear in the official form even when contributed or provisional.

## Labor classification

The official 2027 contracting guide does not provide a universal hourly price. Classify each line according to the real relationship. Schedule, supervision, continuity or dependency indicate a worker relationship and must not be disguised as honorarios. The responsible person's own assignment remains subject to the 20% cap; another person requires a declared role, commitment letter and corresponding labor cost.

If an autonomous honorarios service genuinely applies, use the guide's 16% withholding rate for 2027. If the relationship is employment, include the applicable employer obligations. The label in the spreadsheet does not decide the relationship.
