## Must be checked in the official platform

- Applicant region and the deadline shown for that region.
- Project start and end dates within the allowed 2027 window.
- Official project title, requested amount and final budget categories.
- Required Profile Cultura and curriculum fields.
- Character limits for each artistic, technical and schedule field.
- Mandatory documents shown by the platform for this specific line.
- Confirmation that every attachment is individual, uncompressed and below the platform limit.

## Evidence or authorization still required

- Rights for audio, textures, fonts, footage, logos and any third-party asset.
- Quotations or defensible estimates for luminaires, recording, venue and other substantial costs.
- Final audio choice and its license or authorization.
- Venue or installation feasibility evidence, if the selected presentation requires it.
- Team commitments and labor classification consistent with the real relationship.
- A physical BLE execution record if the final application describes an observed hardware run rather than a funded experiment.

## Deadline rule

The official call index and audiovisual FAQ report 8 September 2026 at 15:00 for ordinary regions and 11 September 2026 at 15:00 for Arica y Parinacota, Tarapaca, Antofagasta and Atacama. An older line-specific page may still show 4 September. The extension resolution and the platform at upload time are authoritative.

## Final integrity check

Before submitting, compare title, dates, amount, duration, device count, deliverables and rights language across the official form, treatment, budget and complementary video. Do not describe the multi-device BLE setup as already completed unless a corresponding run record exists. Preserve the platform receipt, timestamp and folio after submission.
